A reliable Halal partner for your market

Bhadella Import & Export, Lda exports Halal food from Portugal to importers, distributors and wholesalers. We already supply customers in Spain, Angola, Mozambique and other European countries.

Our export range includes the brands we represent — Robert Halal, Freshly, El Maha and Calnort — and our own brand, Bhadella Fresh Products: luncheon meats, sausages, cold cuts, jellies, flans, mousses and stocks.
