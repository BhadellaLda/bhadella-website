Our story

A reference in Halal food representation

Bhadella is a Portuguese Import & Export company. Its core business consists of representing, distributing and manufacturing Halal food product brands.

Throughout our journey, we have worked to consolidate our presence in the national and international markets as a reference company in the representation of Halal food products, structuring our product portfolio to anticipate the needs of our customers since 1999.

“Always there for you”

Mission

To facilitate access to high-quality Halal food by offering reliable and transparent products and services, aligned with the highest international standards, ensuring everyone can enjoy Halal food, no matter where they are.

Vision

To make Bhadella a national and international reference in Halal brand representation and distribution: a rigorous, ethical and inclusive model that builds trust and adds value for our clients, partners and consumers.
