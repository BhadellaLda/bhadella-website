Cook with our products

Simple, tasty ideas with Robert Halal products — most are ready in under 30 minutes.

Sausage croquettes

Serves 430 min

Crispy outside, soft inside — a favourite with children.

See the recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 can of Robert Halal Chicken Sausages (425 g)
  • 500 g cold mashed potato
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 100 g breadcrumbs
  • Oil for frying, salt and pepper

Method

  1. Drain the sausages and pat them dry.
  2. Season the mashed potato and wrap each sausage in a thin layer of it.
  3. Coat in flour, then egg, then breadcrumbs.
  4. Fry in hot oil for 3–4 minutes until golden and drain on kitchen paper.

Product used: Robert Halal Chicken Sausages

Grilled toast

Serves 210 min

A quick, filling breakfast or snack.

See the recipe

Ingredients

  • 4 slices of bread
  • 4 slices of Robert Halal Chicken Luncheon Meat
  • 1 tomato, sliced
  • 4 slices of cheese
  • Fresh basil and chives, olive oil

Method

  1. Toast the bread in a grill pan with a little olive oil.
  2. Top with luncheon meat, tomato and cheese.
  3. Grill or bake for 3–4 minutes until the cheese melts.
  4. Garnish with basil and chives and serve warm.

Product used: Robert Halal Chicken Luncheon Meat

Mixed salad

Serves 215 min

Fresh, colourful and ready in 15 minutes.

See the recipe

Ingredients

  • ½ can of Robert Halal Chicken Luncheon Meat, cut into strips
  • 1 lettuce, 1 tomato and ½ red pepper
  • 1 grated carrot and a handful of green olives
  • Croutons and shavings of hard cheese
  • Olive oil and vinegar to dress

Method

  1. Wash and cut the vegetables.
  2. Arrange the lettuce, tomato, pepper and carrot on a plate.
  3. Add the luncheon meat, olives, croutons and cheese.
  4. Dress just before serving.

Product used: Robert Halal Chicken Luncheon Meat

Sausages and potatoes

Serves 435 min

A simple one-pan family dinner.

See the recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 can of Robert Halal Chicken Sausages (425 g)
  • 600 g small new potatoes
  • 1 red pepper and 1 green pepper
  • 2 spring onions
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, paprika, salt and pepper

Method

  1. Boil the potatoes for 15–20 minutes until tender, then cut in half.
  2. Slice the sausages and cut the peppers into pieces.
  3. Sauté the peppers in olive oil for 5 minutes, add the potatoes, sausages and paprika and cook for 5 more minutes.
  4. Sprinkle with chopped spring onion and serve.

Product used: Robert Halal Chicken Sausages

Courgette bites

Serves 415 min

A light, fresh starter — no cooking needed.

See the recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 large courgette

  • 6 slices of Robert Halal Chicken Luncheon Meat
  • 1 small red chilli, finely chopped
  • Fresh mint leaves

Method

  4. Finish with chilli and mint and serve chilled.

Chilli potato cake

Serves 460 min

Layers of oven-baked potato with a gentle kick.

See the recipe

Ingredients

  • 800 g potatoes, thinly sliced
  • 1 onion, thinly sliced
  • 200 ml cooking cream and 1 egg
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes, salt and pepper
  • 1 can of Robert Halal Chicken Luncheon Meat Hot Spiced, sliced

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 190 °C and grease an ovenproof dish.
  2. Layer the potato and onion in the dish, seasoning as you go.
  3. Whisk the cream, egg and chilli and pour over the potatoes.
  4. Bake for 45–50 minutes until golden. Cut into squares and top with slices of luncheon meat.

Product used: Robert Halal Chicken Luncheon Meat Hot Spiced

Ali Nazik

Serves 440 min

A classic Turkish dish of smoky aubergine and garlic yogurt.

See the recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 aubergines
  • 300 g natural yogurt and 1 garlic clove
  • ½ can of Robert Halal Chicken Luncheon Meat, cubed
  • 2 green chillies
  • 1 tbsp butter, salt and fresh parsley

Method

  1. Roast the aubergines for about 25 minutes until soft, then peel and chop.
  2. Mix the yogurt with crushed garlic and a pinch of salt.
  3. Fry the luncheon meat and chillies in butter for 3–4 minutes.
  4. Spread the aubergine on a plate, top with yogurt, then the luncheon meat and chillies.

Product used: Robert Halal Chicken Luncheon Meat

Luncheon meat pancakes

Serves 425 min

Savoury pancakes for a weekend brunch.

See the recipe

Ingredients

  • 200 g flour and a pinch of salt
  • 2 eggs and 300 ml milk
  • 100 g of Robert Halal Chicken Luncheon Meat, finely diced
  • Butter for the pan
  • Cottage cheese and fresh fruit to serve

Method

  1. Whisk the flour, salt, eggs and milk into a smooth batter.
  2. Stir in the diced luncheon meat.
  3. Cook ladlefuls in a buttered pan for about 2 minutes on each side.
  4. Serve with cottage cheese and fresh fruit.

Product used: Robert Halal Chicken Luncheon Meat
